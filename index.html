GTM Control Tower: which leads can safely enter the CRM?

Messy exports can contain duplicate people, invalid emails, missing owners, and conflicting lifecycle stages. Control Tower applies explicit rules and keeps unresolved records out of the destination-ready set.

Try it: run the 64-row cleanup. The batch becomes 56 contacts after eight duplicate rows merge: 44 are ready and 12 remain held. Compare a duplicate and an invalid email in the source preview, then use the separate CSV audit to download a readiness report.

Design decision: an unresolved record stays visible for review. The browser example uses a fixed routing rule and a supplied expected stage; it does not infer historical truth or write to a CRM.

Pomade: turn account data into inspectable research

Research results need context before they become sales inputs. Pomade combines a data grid, reusable recipes, source-linked answers, and deliberate export or CRM actions.

Inspect it: the September 7 provider benchmark records 12 provider-accepted work emails from 15 test contacts, including three additional results from fallback. It also records development-CRM read-back and an unchanged repeat preview. These are results for that sample, not a general accuracy or coverage claim.

Design decision: keep raw responses, sources, and uncertainty available for review. Successful execution and a citation do not establish that a claim is correct. Hosted access remains private; provider workflows require configured accounts.

Hot Potato: explain who receives an inbound lead

Routing should account for an eligible existing owner, rep availability, and assignment weights. The engine makes those rules inspectable and reuses the decision for a repeated request ID.

Inspect it: the routing walkthrough links to the public implementation and tests for owner preference, weighted assignment, and an unavailable pool.

Design decision: show the assignment and its limits. The website plays preset fictional results; it does not check a calendar or book a meeting. Historical rule snapshots are not implemented.
